Questions. Before you sell to me

The stuff people usually ask. If yours isn't here, ask me on Discord.

Answers

How do I get a price?

Join my Discord, open a ticket and paste the cert numbers from your slabs. I'll send one price for the whole stack, usually within a few minutes.

Do you give a price for each card?

No, I give one price for everything you send together. You can say yes or no to that.

Is there a minimum?

I'm looking for slabs worth $50 or more each. Even one card is fine.

How do I get paid?

Zelle when your slabs arrive and match the certs in your ticket. If you'd rather, PayPal Goods & Services, and you cover the PayPal fee.

Do I have to take the price?

No. Asking is free. If it doesn't work for you, keep your cards.

How do I send the slabs?

Ship them to me once you say yes. I send the address privately in your ticket. You pay shipping, and the package needs to arrive by Monday.

Which graders do you buy?

PSA, CGC and BGS. For CGC or BGS a clear photo of the label helps too.

How long is the price good for?

Prices move, so it's for now. If you need a few days to think, tell me and I'll let you know if it still stands.

Can I sell only some of the cards?

Sure. Tell me which ones you want to keep and I'll give you a new price for the rest.

How it works

  1. Send certs

    Join my Discord, open a ticket and paste the cert numbers from your slabs.

  2. Get one price

    I look them up and send back one price for everything. Usually takes a few minutes.

  3. Ship and get paid

    If it works for you, I send the shipping address privately in your ticket. Ship them to me, I pay Zelle when they arrive and match (or PayPal G&S, you cover the fee). You pay shipping. Arrives by Monday.
