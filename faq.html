Questions. Before you sell to me

The stuff people usually ask. If yours isn't here, ask me on Discord.

Answers

How do I get a price?

Join my Discord, open a ticket and paste the cert numbers from your slabs, or post clear photos of the labels. I'll send one price for the whole stack, usually within a few minutes.

What do I do once I'm in the Discord?

Open a ticket (the how-to-sell channel shows where). In your ticket, paste your cert numbers or post photos of the slab labels. The bot replies with the numbers it read so you can fix any that are wrong, then I send you one offer for the whole stack. Say yes and I send the shipping address privately in your ticket.

Can I just send photos instead of typing the certs?

Yes. Post clear photos of the labels, straight on and without glare, several slabs per photo is fine. The bot reads the cert numbers and shows you what it read. If it can't read one, type that number.

How do I get paid?

Zelle when your slabs arrive and match the certs in your ticket. If you'd rather, PayPal Goods & Services, and you cover the PayPal fee.

Do I have to take the price?

No. Asking is free. If it doesn't work for you, keep your cards.

How do I send the slabs?

Ship them to me once you say yes. I send the address privately in your ticket. You pay shipping, and the package needs to arrive by Monday.

Which graders do you buy?

PSA, CGC and BGS. For CGC or BGS a clear photo of the label helps too.

How long is the price good for?

Prices move, so it's for now. If you need a few days to think, tell me and I'll let you know if it still stands.

Can I sell only some of the cards?

Sure. Tell me which ones you want to keep and I'll give you a new price for the rest.

Do you pay for referrals?

Yes. Send me a seller: when they sell to me, you get $10 added to your next offer. They just tag you in their ticket, like "ref @you".
