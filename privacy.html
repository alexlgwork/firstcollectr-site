Privacy. What I keep and why

Short version: this site tracks nothing, and what you send me to sell your cards is only used for that.

This website

No cookies, no analytics, no ads and no trackers. The pages are plain files. The host that serves them (GitHub Pages) may keep the usual server logs any website host keeps.

When you sell to me

What you send me in a Discord ticket (cert numbers, photos, and the details I need to ship and pay you) is used only to price, receive and pay for your cards. I don't sell it or share it with anyone, except what Zelle, PayPal or the shipping carrier need to do their part.

My own tools

I run my business with a private app of my own. When I connect it to my own Google account, it reads and writes my own spreadsheets and my own email receipts, for my bookkeeping. It only works with my account, never anyone else's Google data, and nothing it reads is sold or shared.

Questions about any of this? Ask me on Discord.

How it works

  1. Send certs

    Join my Discord, open a ticket and paste the cert numbers from your slabs.

  2. Get one price

    I look them up and send back one price for everything. Usually takes a few minutes.

  3. Ship and get paid

    If it works for you, I send the shipping address privately in your ticket. Ship them to me, I pay Zelle when they arrive and match (or PayPal G&S, you cover the fee). You pay shipping. Arrives by Monday.

Don't like the number? No problem. You keep your cards.
