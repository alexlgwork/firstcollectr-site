How it works

  1. Send certs

    Join my Discord, open a ticket and paste the cert numbers from your slabs.

  2. Get one price

    I look them up and send back one price for everything. Usually takes a few minutes.

    If it works for you, I send the shipping address privately in your ticket. Ship them to me, I pay Zelle when they arrive and match (or PayPal G&S, you cover the fee). You pay shipping. Arrives by Monday.
